Who we are

A Nigerian construction company built on delivery

Boni-Chivila Global Services Limited is a private limited company incorporated in Nigeria in November 2022 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act. Headquartered in Garki, Abuja, we take on civil, building and energy projects for government agencies, private developers and communities.

  • Owned and deployed heavy equipment: graders, loaders, excavators and tippers
  • Hands-on site supervision from earthworks to finishing
  • Fully registered, with stamp-duty-certified incorporation documents
